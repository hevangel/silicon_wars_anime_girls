# Silicon Wars: series bible

A seinen technology fantasy about corporate personifications whose powers derive from real computing specialties. The central rule: even the strongest AI queen depends on compute, bandwidth, foundries, equipment and verification.

## Character ages
- **Elementary-school child prodigies:** Cerebras, Mistral AI, DeepSeek. All designs and stories are wholesome and nonsexualized.
- **Mature executives:** IBM and Oracle. IBM is the silver-haired Iron Lady who survives every technological cycle.
- **Very old grandmothers:** Bell Labs (scholarly research grandmother), Fairchild (hands-on semiconductor matriarch).
- **Younger grannies:** DEC (red varsity jacket, interactive-computing hacker), Sun Microsystems (joyful purple Solaris jacket).

## Strict visual continuity
- Intel: long light-blue hair, pointed sapphire crown with a blue oval Intel-logo badge, and a royal-blue imperial uniform with gold-fringed epaulettes. Composed, authoritative pose.
- AMD: fiery red ponytail, red/black chiplet armor, athletic challenger stance.
- Nvidia, the Emerald Queen: thick emerald-green braid and a black leather jacket-dress with glowing green circuit seams. She has the greatest raw physical strength of the cast, with powerful arms and naturally proportioned athletic legs.
- Fairchild: rolled sleeves, red bandana, glasses, working forewoman; do not duplicate Bell Labs' silhouette.
- Bell Labs: gentle elderly scholar, tweed and vintage instruments.
- DEC and Sun: preserve the selected designs from the final four-grandmothers infographic.

## Season 1 story threads

The [Season 1 character index](../characters/INDEX.md) defines the cast and each tier.

1. **Compute Triad.** Intel, AMD, and Nvidia anchor the season.
2. **Platforms.** Google, Microsoft, Apple, Meta, and Amazon are the first supporting group.
3. **AI labs.** Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Hugging Face, Mistral AI, and DeepSeek are the second supporting group.
4. **Foundry, equipment, and memory.** TSMC, ASML, Samsung, Micron, and SK hynix supply the manufacturing and memory threads.
5. **Other chip companies.** Arm, Qualcomm, Broadcom, Marvell, Huawei, and Cerebras round out the chip cast.
6. **Cameos.** IBM, Oracle, Sun Microsystems, Bell Labs, Fairchild Semiconductor, DEC, and Xerox PARC appear in smaller roles.

The stories are fictional metaphors, not depictions of real employees or official product claims.
