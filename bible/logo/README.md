# Silicon Wars logo trace

[silicon_wars_logo_trace.svg](silicon_wars_logo_trace.svg) is a black-line, white-background trace of the SILICON WARS wordmark: two lines, an angular italic display face, outlined letters.

- Source: a low-resolution (226×119) photo of the title on a screen. The photo is not stored in the repo.
- Method: the letters were rebuilt as clean vector outlines and fitted against the photo by eye, not auto-traced, because the photo is too small and blurry to trace without noise.
- Not included: the sponsor mark above the title in the photo, and the neon glow and inner glow line. This file is the wordmark only.
- Limits: letter shapes are close, not exact. The decorative downward spikes under "WARS" in the photo are not reproduced. Replace this with a trace of a higher-resolution source if one becomes available.
