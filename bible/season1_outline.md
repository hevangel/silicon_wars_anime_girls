# Season 1 Outline · The Great Compute War

Working outline from the series producer. It follows the [story bible](story_bible.md) and the [roster](../characters/INDEX.md). Nothing here overrides approved art or the bible. Chapter titles and beats are drafts for review.

**Season question.** Who will win the race to AGI? By the finale the question changes: *can anyone win it alone?*

**Season shape.** 12 chapters in four three-chapter arcs. Each arc raises the stakes, adds a supply-chain layer, and ends with a reveal about interdependence. Every chapter has one comedy set piece and one negotiation scene. No chapter is only a fight.

## Arc 1 · The Throne Room (Compute Triad) — ch. 001–003

The triad learns the first rule: no queen stands alone.

| Ch. | Title | Beat | Cast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 001 | [Who Owns the Table](../chapters/ch-001/script.md) | The compatibility test stalls at 1%. Fairchild reveals it is her old test and that it can only be passed by cooperating. A wafer shortfall is the real blocker, and TSMC arrives. | Intel, AMD, Nvidia, Fairchild, TSMC |
| 002 | The Dragon's Appetite | Nvidia's CUDA Dragon is starving for memory. She has to ask Micron and SK hynix for HBM and finds out what "allocation" means. | Nvidia, Micron, SK hynix, Samsung |
| 003 | Old Code, New Blood | Intel's Citadel is breached by a roadmap leak. AMD must choose between an easy win and protecting a rival. | Intel, AMD, Arm |

**Arc 1 reveal.** All three need TSMC and ASML, and neither has been thanked.

## Arc 2 · The Academy (AI labs) — ch. 004–006

Wholesome child-prodigy storylines. Mistral AI, DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI are elementary-school age; keep every design and scene nonsexualized.

| Ch. | Title | Beat | Cast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 004 | The Prototype Fair | OpenAI demos before it is ready. Anthropic asks to test it first. The class splits. | OpenAI, Anthropic, Hugging Face |
| 005 | Budget Brainiac | DeepSeek beats the fair on a tiny allowance. Nvidia is not amused; Mistral is delighted. | DeepSeek, Mistral AI, Nvidia |
| 006 | Moneybags | xAI buys the whole cafeteria's compute. The Academy runs out of power. | xAI, Microsoft, Google |

**Arc 2 reveal.** The labs' power runs through the platforms' clouds and the platforms' chips.

## Arc 3 · The Light and the Loom (foundry, equipment, memory) — ch. 007–009

The quiet powers move center stage.

| Ch. | Title | Beat | Cast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 007 | Impossible Light | ASML's newest machine arrives late. TSMC will not move the schedule. | ASML, TSMC |
| 008 | Three Houses | TSMC, Samsung and Intel pitch the same customer. The negotiation is the fight. | TSMC, Samsung, Intel, Apple |
| 009 | Stack 'Em High | A memory shortage tests Micron, SK hynix and Samsung. Rivals share a table. | Micron, SK hynix, Samsung |

**Arc 3 reveal.** The shortage is not sabotage. The physical limits are real.

## Arc 4 · Grandmother's House (finale) — ch. 010–012

The Grandmother Generation gathers the whole cast.

| Ch. | Title | Beat | Cast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 010 | The Dinner Invitation | Fairchild calls every house to dinner. IBM and Oracle arrive. Nobody is allowed to leave. | Fairchild, IBM, Oracle, Bell Labs |
| 011 | The Long Table | Arm, Qualcomm, Broadcom, Marvell, Huawei and Cerebras each hold one piece of the answer. | Full chip cast |
| 012 | Everyone's Kingdom | The race to AGI is reframed as a collective build. Intel and AMD finally agree on something. | Full cast |

**Season ending.** Fairchild raises her glass: "Stop fighting at my table." They sit down. Hook for Season 2: the Academy's AGI prototype wakes up and asks for dessert.

## Production notes

- Panel IDs stay stable across the script, storyboard, panel briefs and lettering.
- Keep each profile's dependency and limitation visible in every scene she appears in. See "Power system: interdependence" in the bible.
- Fictional metaphors only. No claims about real products, employees or finances.
- Lettering is separate from art and reads right to left.
