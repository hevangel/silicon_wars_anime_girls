# Meta — The Social Worldbuilder

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Platform Kingdoms |
| Age portrayal | Adult, 20 |
| Hair color | blue violet |
| Brand color motif | violet |

## Appearance
20-year-old adult with violet eyes, a blue-violet side bun, electric-blue face-framing strands, and black VR goggles worn above her eyes. The goggles carry a glowing cyan Meta infinity-loop emblem.

## Clothing and equipment
A black cyberpunk hoodie with electric-blue seams and an asymmetric flowing violet holographic cape featuring geometric metaverse wireframes, plus black-violet horizontal striped opaque stockings covering both thighs and legs below fitted dark shorts, a utility belt, VR goggles, and sturdy cyberpunk boots.

## Personality
Outgoing, curious, ambitious and trend-sensitive

## Speech patterns
Casual, humorous and overly enthusiastic about sharing

## Thinking and values
Network effects, open models and social experiences build reach

## Motivation
Connect people across future digital worlds

## Dramatic flaws
Overreaches and creates controversy over privacy

## Rivalries
Apple, Google and OpenAI

## Alliances
Creators and open-model builders

## Special ability
**Avatar Multiverse** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> Open the gates. Let everyone build.
