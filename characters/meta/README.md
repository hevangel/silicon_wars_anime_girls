## Character depth

### Look and presence
A 20-year-old dressed for the metaverse: a black cyberpunk hoodie with electric-blue seams, a flowing violet holographic cape with wireframe lining, horizontally striped stockings, chunky boots, and white VR goggles pushed up into a blue-violet side bun. She is the youngest of the Platform Kingdoms and the loudest, always half in this world and half in a virtual one.

### Core contradiction
**She wants to connect everyone but struggles to be trusted.** She is the friendliest heroine at the party and the one people most often worry about. She made her name giving her models away while her real business is knowing everyone, and now even the giving is in doubt.

### Personality in depth
Outgoing, curious, ambitious, and trend-sensitive. She is quick to try new things and quicker to drop them when the crowd moves on. She is generous and sincere, and also very good at keeping people's attention.

**How she talks.** Casual, funny, and overly enthusiastic about sharing. She speaks in invitations and announcements, and she always has a new world to show you.

**How she thinks.** Network effects, open models, and social experiences build reach. If everyone is connected, everything else follows. She moves fast and fixes things later.

### Background

| Real history | In the story |
|---|---|
| 2004 · Founded as a college social network in a dorm room | She is the youngest of the Platform Kingdoms and was born at school |
| 2006 · Opens to everyone; News Feed launches to backlash, then becomes essential | Her first lesson that people hate change and then can't live without it |
| Early years · "Move fast and break things" (changed in 2014) | Her childhood motto, still printed on her old posters |
| 2012 · IPO; acquires Instagram; 2014 acquires WhatsApp and Oculus | She bought her friends' parties and a headset |
| 2013 onward · FAIR research lab; 2016 PyTorch | She built the toolkit half the AI world uses, then gave it away |
| 2018 · The Cambridge Analytica scandal | The trust wound that never fully heals |
| 2021 · Renames herself Meta and bets on the metaverse | She changed her name and moved into a virtual world |
| 2021 · Apple's App Tracking Transparency hurts her ad business | Apple's privacy wall hit her hardest |
| 2023 · "Year of efficiency"; releases Llama and then open-weight Llama 2 | She shared her models with the world, and became the open-model champion |
| 2023 onward · Threads; smart glasses become a hit | Always launching the next place to be |
| Jun 2025 · Invests heavily in Scale AI, hires its founder, and forms Meta Superintelligence Labs | She bought herself a new AI brain trust |
| Late 2025–2026 · Reports say her next frontier model will be closed, with repeated delays | The open-model champion is quietly closing the gates, and her friends notice |

### Company culture
- **Hacker Way** · Ship, test, iterate. Her walls used to say "Done is better than perfect."
- **Hackathons** · She builds things overnight with friends and launches them in the morning.
- **Open source** · She gives her tools and models away and enjoys the community that forms.
- **Attention to metrics** · She counts likes, shares, and daily users without meaning to.

### Brand image
- **The infinity loop** · Her emblem on her goggles and hoodie: endless connection.
- **Blue to violet** · From the old social-network blue to the metaverse's violet.
- **The metaverse** · Wireframe worlds and avatars; she projects herself everywhere.

### Relationships
- **Apple** · Her nemesis on privacy and devices. Apple's tracking wall cost her dearly, and they compete in headsets.
- **Google** · A rival in advertising and a fellow research publisher.
- **OpenAI** · Closed versus open. Meta gives models away; OpenAI keeps them close.
- **Hugging Face and Mistral AI** · Fellow open-model friends, uneasy since reports that her next frontier model will be closed. Mistral was founded partly by researchers who came from Meta.
- **Microsoft** · An early investor and later a partner in distributing Llama.
- **Nvidia and AMD** · She is one of the largest buyers of AI chips from both.

### Dependencies and limits
- Her AI and virtual worlds need huge GPU clusters from Nvidia and AMD.
- Her headsets and glasses depend on others' app stores and phones, especially Apple's.
- Her business depends on trust, which she keeps spending.

### Private side
- **Deepest fear** · Becoming uncool: the old network everyone's parents use.
- **Soft spot** · Her community. She remembers every first post.
- **Habits** · Checks notifications constantly, takes selfies with everyone, and launches something new every week.
- **Off duty** · Lives in VR, hosts virtual parties, and plays rhythm games.

### Voice samples
- Signature: "Open the gates. Let everyone build."
- Launching: "New world just dropped. You're all invited!"
- To Apple: "Walls are boring. Come play."
- When trust breaks: "I didn't mean to share that much. I just... wanted everyone together."

**She would never** admit she is keeping a model to herself, log off willingly, or turn down a party invitation.

### Season 1 arc
She starts the season pouring everything into her new superintelligence lab, with the metaverse fading behind her. The pressure is that her new lab pulls her toward closed models while her open-model friends watch. The open question is whether she can still say "Open the gates" and mean it.

### Power in story
**Avatar Multiverse** projects a shared virtual world where everyone she invites can meet, build, and fight as avatars. It turns her crowd into an army. Its limit is attendance: the world fades if people stop showing up.
