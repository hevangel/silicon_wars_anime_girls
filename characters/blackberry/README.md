## Personality and voice
Short, precise, confidential, impatient with unfocused chatter. **Worldview:** The most valuable message is the one delivered immediately and safely. **Motivation:** Protect the trusted business network and keep decision-makers connected. **Contradiction:** she can be extraordinarily competent yet dismisses touch-first consumer software experiences as distractions.

## Relationships and limits
- **Rivals:** Nokia, Palm and later Apple's smartphone incarnation.
- **Allies:** Corporate IT stewards, wireless operators, regulated-industry customers.
- **Dependencies:** Carrier data connectivity, messaging servers, enterprise policies, trusted infrastructure and compatible handsets.
- **Power:** **Push-Mail Dispatch**, an invented visual expression of the company's role.
- **Limit:** Secure delivery does not by itself create a compelling app marketplace or touch UX.
- **Season 2 arc:** From indispensable private courier to a leader struggling with a newly consumer-driven pocket kingdom; her competence never vanishes when her position changes.
