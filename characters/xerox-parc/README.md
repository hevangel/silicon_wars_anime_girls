# Xerox PARC — The Forgotten Inventor

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Grandmother Generation |
| Age portrayal | elder |
| Hair color | white |

## Appearance
Frailty disguises a quick mind; elderly white-haired inventor with gentle blue eyes

## Clothing and equipment
Research cardigan, old mouse necklace and sketchbook of GUIs

## Personality
Inventive, kind, melancholy and brilliant

## Speech patterns
Treats revolutionary ideas as ordinary observations

## Thinking and values
Human-centered interfaces make computing usable

## Motivation
Ensure fundamental interface ideas are remembered

## Dramatic flaws
Poor at commercializing original discoveries

## Rivalries
Companies that commercialized her inventions

## Alliances
Apple, Microsoft and networking researchers

## Special ability
**Window of Possibilities** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> Oh yes dear, we tried that years ago.
