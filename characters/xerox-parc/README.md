# Xerox PARC — The Unpaid Inventor

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Grandmother Generation |
| Age portrayal | Elder, early seventies |
| Hair color | White |
| Brand color motif | Dusty rose |

## Appearance

Lean inventor in her early seventies with a side-parted white bob, lined face, and attentive blue-gray eyes. She looks older than IBM and Oracle, with a slight stoop and a softer bearing than Fairchild.

## Clothing and equipment

Faded dusty-rose collared dress, mended oatmeal cardigan, vintage mouse pendant, tan stockings, and scuffed brown loafers. Her paper tower contains years of prototype listings and diagrams. She works at a salvaged Alto with a dim display and a printer that barely has toner.

## Personality

Generous with prototypes, dryly funny about credit, and still thrilled when a new experiment works. Even DeepSeek has more to work with. Xerox takes notes on the backs of rejected grant applications and will still hand her last clean sheet to someone with a good idea.

## Speech patterns

Treats revolutionary ideas as ordinary observations and backs up her claims with dated printouts.

## Thinking and values

A good interface puts the person using it first. A working prototype is worth more than a sales pitch.

## Motivation

Keep her lab working on a shoestring and make sure its ideas are remembered.

## Dramatic flaws

Shows everyone her prototypes before she thinks to ask for credit or funding.

## Rivalries

Apple and Microsoft when they announce familiar ideas as breakthroughs.

## Alliances

Apple, Microsoft, and networking researchers when there is a problem worth solving.

## Special ability

**Window of Possibilities** — she turns a sketch into an interactive prototype. She can demonstrate what it would do, but she still needs parts, funding, and collaborators to build it at scale.

## Signature line

> Oh yes, dear. We tried that years ago. Turn it over. The fix is on the back.
