## Appearance
Copper-gold hair, green eyes and a field of floating atoms

## Clothing and equipment
White scientific alchemist robe, molecular gloves and layered gold jewelry

## Personality
Inventive, patient, fascinated by material transformations

## Speech patterns
Elegant chemistry metaphors and excited technical explanations

## Thinking and values
Precise processing of matter creates devices that physics allows

## Motivation
Master next-generation materials processing

## Dramatic flaws
Can pursue beauty at the cost of practical manufacturing

## Rivalries
Lam Research and Tokyo Electron

## Alliances
Foundries and ASML

## Special ability
**Atomic Deposition** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
