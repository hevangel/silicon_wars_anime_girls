# DeepSeek — The Budget Brainiac

> An original *Silicon Wars* company personification. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | AI Academy Juniors |
| Age portrayal | Elementary-school child, about 9–10 |
| Hair color | Ocean blue, with navy roots and sea-blue tips |
| Brand color motif | Royal blue, sea blue, and white |

## Look and presence

Her ocean-blue hair is swept into two neat round buns, each pinned with a small whale tail. She wears a royal-blue qipao with white piping, frog-knot closures, and whale embroidery, with white knee socks and blue cloth Mary Janes. The dress has a neatly let-down hem. A pencil lives behind her ear, and the old price tag still hangs from her backpack with the price crossed out.

At her desk, she props up her chin with one hand and moves the abacus beads with the other. Her raised eyebrow and little knowing smile mean she's spotted a shortcut. Her battered whale-logo laptop sits beside a single old GPU cushioned in a bento box.

## Core contradiction

**Frugal and proud, tired of being called cheap.** She can match a bigger setup with one old card and a better idea. She shares the trick freely, then goes quiet when someone treats the result as a fluke. She's quick to make do and slow to admit when some help would actually make a difference.

## Habits and voice

- She bows a little when she greets someone and looks down at her hands when the class praises her.
- Under pressure, she squints, recalculates, and says exactly what she found. “I can do it a cheaper way.”
- She writes neat diagrams late into the night, forgets her tea, and pins her notes to the class bulletin board for anyone to copy.

## Relationships

- [OpenAI](../openai/README.md) sizes up the quiet transfer student after DeepSeek aces a test with half the study time.
- [xAI](../xai/README.md) buys the GPU stock. DeepSeek takes one hand-me-down card and gets to work. She quietly enjoys the moment xAI realizes how close their results are.
- Anthropic and Google are also rivals. She shares what she learns with researchers and open-model builders.

## Signature power

**Sparse Awakening** makes a blue whale holding a lightbulb appear beside her tally of resources. The GPU bar stays short while the result bar climbs.

> I found a simpler answer.
