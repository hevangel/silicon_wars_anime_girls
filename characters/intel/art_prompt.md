## Continuity lock

- **Face and hair** · Composed, commanding face, sapphire-blue eyes, and long flowing light-blue hair with icy sky-blue highlights and soft blue shadows.
- **Crown and jewelry** · Pointed metallic crown set with faceted sapphire-blue crystals, gold trim, and sapphire drop earrings. Keep the original gold brow band and sapphire arrangement free of lettering.
- **Uniform** · High-collared royal-blue imperial military coat, cream-white front and lining, gold-fringed epaulettes, layered gold cords, tailored double-breasted waist, and long split panels embroidered with subtle blue circuitry. White gloves and tall dark military boots. Embroider one small ivory Intel Inside emblem above the gold hem at the lower outer corner of her left front skirt panel.
- **Body language and prop** · Disciplined and poised. Use a wafer-shaped heirloom when the scene calls for it. Her signature power is x86 Citadel.

For a sheet, render front, three-quarter, side, and back views at the same scale, with the crown tips and boots visible. Include composed, stern, and surprised expressions and a small color palette. For the reference card, frame her full length so the crown and skirt emblem are visible. Use detailed manga linework and cel shading.

Signature line · “I built this kingdom before you counted transistors.”
