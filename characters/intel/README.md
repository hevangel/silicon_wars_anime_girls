# Intel — The Blue Empress

*Silicon Wars* fictional company personification. This fan-created character is not affiliated with the real company.

**Faction:** Silicon Kingdoms  
**Age portrayal:** adult  
**Hair:** light blue\
**Signature color:** royal blue

## Appearance
Composed and commanding; sapphire blue eyes and long flowing light-blue hair.

## Clothing and accessories
Sapphire-crystal crown, royal-blue imperial military coat with gold-fringed epaulettes and cream lining, white gloves, and a wafer-shaped heirloom.

## Personality
Proud, disciplined, strategic, wary of decline.

## How she speaks
Formal and imperious, with dry references to computing history.

## How she thinks
Control of x86 compatibility, manufacturing know-how and enduring institutional power.

## Motivation
Reclaim technological leadership across processors and foundries.

## Flaw
Often relies on legacy power and resists abrupt changes.

## Rivals
AMD, Nvidia.

## Allies
IBM and the PC ecosystem.

## Signature power
**x86 Citadel** — a fictional dramatization of the real company's technology.

## Signature dialogue
> I built this kingdom before you counted transistors.
