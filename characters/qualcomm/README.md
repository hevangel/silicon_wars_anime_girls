# Qualcomm — The Last-Mile Ace

*Silicon Wars* fictional company personification. This fan-created character is unaffiliated with the real company.

- **Faction** — Connectivity Quartet
- **Age portrayal** — 19–20-year-old young adult
- **Hair** — Electric cobalt
- **Signature color** — Cobalt

## Appearance

A lean, athletic dispatch rider with bright blue eyes and a long electric-blue ponytail tied high with a gold band. Her usual sideways grin gives way to a steady stare when a signal starts to break up.

## Clothing and accessories

A cobalt rainproof jacket with an asymmetric gold zipper, hood worn down, a white shoulder panel bearing the Qualcomm wordmark, and a black chest strap with a red-and-white Snapdragon clasp. She wears graphite utility trousers, white-and-cobalt riding sneakers, and a hard-shell kit for spare relay nodes at her hip. She wears one cobalt Bluetooth earbud and keeps her other ear free to hear the street.

## Personality

Qualcomm is impatient with systems and patient with people. She brags about her arrival times and volunteers before the briefing ends. If somebody drops off a group call, she's the first to stop and check on them. She taps her earbud twice after each handoff and waits for an answer. She takes pride in knowing which rooftops have backup power and gets prickly when someone calls her “just the courier.”

## How she speaks

Quick banter about routes, signal strength, and battery life. When things get serious, she gets quiet and keeps her words short.

## How she thinks

She looks for the shortest route that keeps a call live and leaves enough battery for a reply.

## Motivation

Get the message through, especially to the person everyone else has written off as out of range.

## Flaw

She commits to shortcuts before hearing the full briefing and has trouble trusting someone else to finish a run she promised to do.

## Rivals

MediaTek, Apple, and Samsung mobile silicon.

## Allies

Arm, Broadcom, and device makers.

## Signature power

**Snapdragon Dash** carries a live connection from one relay node to the next as she moves. If there is no working node within reach, she has to stop and plant one herself.

## Signature dialogue

> Hold up. We lost somebody.
