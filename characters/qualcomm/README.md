# Qualcomm — The Wireless Speedster

*Silicon Wars* fictional company personification. This fan-created character is not affiliated with the real company.

**Faction:** Connectivity Quartet  
**Age portrayal:** adult  
**Hair:** electric blue  
**Signature color:** cobalt

## Appearance
Electric-blue ponytail, bright eyes and eager energetic smile.

## Clothing and accessories
Blue racing jacket, high-tech sneakers, antenna jewelry and wireless headset.

## Personality
Fast-talking, competitive, ingenious and playful.

## How she speaks
Jokes about signal strength, latency and cables.

## How she thinks
Performance per watt, modems and wireless integration shape mobile computing.

## Motivation
Connect devices at high speed using less power.

## Flaw
Impatience and handset-centric tunnel vision.

## Rivals
MediaTek, Apple and Samsung mobile silicon.

## Allies
Arm and device makers.

## Signature power
**Snapdragon Dash** — a fictional dramatization of the real company's technology.

## Signature dialogue
> You still need a cable? Seriously?
