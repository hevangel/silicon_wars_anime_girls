# Qualcomm — The Last-Mile Ace

Use the [model sheet](character_sheet.png) for costume and proportions and the [reference card](reference_card.jpg) for staging and lighting.

Qualcomm is a 19–20-year-old dispatch rider with a lean athletic build, bright blue eyes, and a long electric-blue high ponytail secured by a gold band. Keep one small cobalt Bluetooth earbud on the side shown on the model sheet. Leave the other ear bare.

Her hood-down cobalt rain jacket has an off-center gold zipper, a white shoulder panel with a Qualcomm wordmark, and a black chest strap with a red-and-white Snapdragon clasp. She wears graphite utility trousers, white-and-cobalt riding sneakers, and a small hard-shell relay kit at her hip.

For the portrait, show her crouched by the railing on a rain-slick station roof at blue hour, one index finger on the earbud and her other hand steadying her on the rail beside a clipped relay. Both eyes are open and a relieved sideways grin is starting to show. A thin cobalt route lights up in the distance. Keep the portrait nameplate centered on two lines, QUALCOMM and THE LAST-MILE ACE, within the standard gold frame.

Use the [locked sheet layouts](../_template/README.md) and the blue Qualcomm wordmark title lockup. For chibi, keep her ponytail, single earbud, shoulder panel, clasp, and hip kit recognizable at roughly 2.5 anatomical head heights.
