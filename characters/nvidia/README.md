# Nvidia — The Emerald Dragon Queen

*Silicon Wars* fictional company personification. This fan-created character is not affiliated with the real company.

**Faction:** Silicon Kingdoms  
**Age portrayal:** adult  
**Hair:** emerald green  
**Signature color:** neon green

## Appearance
Long vivid green hair, piercing emerald eyes and proud dragon-queen bearing.

## Clothing and accessories
Black-and-green ornate armor, floating GPU plates and spectral dragon halo.

## Personality
Charismatic, ambitious, dominant and theatrical.

## How she speaks
Smooth, commanding and teasing; talks as though she already owns the table.

## How she thinks
Software ecosystems multiply the value of accelerated compute.

## Motivation
Define the AI computing platform and its software language.

## Flaw
Arrogance and dependence on suppliers and infrastructure.

## Rivals
AMD, Cerebras.

## Allies
TSMC, memory suppliers and AI labs.

## Signature power
**CUDA Dragon** — a fictional dramatization of the real company's technology.

## Signature dialogue
> Challenge me if you wish. Which of my GPUs are you using?
