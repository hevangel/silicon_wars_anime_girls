# Broadcom — The Network Spider Queen

*Silicon Wars* fictional company personification. This fan-created character is not affiliated with the real company.

**Faction:** Connectivity Quartet  
**Age portrayal:** adult  
**Hair:** dark violet  
**Signature color:** deep red

## Appearance
Dark purple hair, rectangular glasses and an intimidating executive stare.

## Clothing and accessories
Impeccable dark-plum suit and fiber-optic web jewelry.

## Personality
Cold, calculating and formidable in negotiation.

## How she speaks
Concise business language; every conversation feels contractual.

## How she thinks
Networks, infrastructure and switching create strategic leverage.

## Motivation
Make her systems indispensable to infrastructure operators.

## Flaw
Can prioritize deals over invention.

## Rivals
Marvell and Nvidia networking.

## Allies
Enterprises and cloud customers.

## Signature power
**Optical Web** — a fictional dramatization of the real company's technology.

## Signature dialogue
> Your empire runs through my switches.
