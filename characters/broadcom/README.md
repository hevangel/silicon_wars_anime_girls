# Broadcom — The Network Spider Queen

*Silicon Wars* fictional company personification. This fan-created character is unaffiliated with the real company.

- **Faction:** Connectivity Quartet
- **Age portrayal:** adult
- **Hair:** black cherry
- **Signature color:** deep red

## Appearance

Composed adult woman with burgundy eyes, narrow rectangular glasses, and a sculpted black-cherry updo held by two gold fiber-connector hairpins.

## Clothing and accessories

Sharp-shouldered black-plum brocade jacket with gold piping and a red-and-white Broadcom pulse clasp, ankle-length wine-red pleated skirt, gold drop earrings, and black heeled ankle boots.

## Personality

Composed under pressure, precise, quietly intimidating, and formidable in negotiation. During an outage she restores the connection before discussing terms. She remembers exactly who needed her.

## How she speaks

Concise business language. Every conversation feels contractual.

## How she thinks

Networks, infrastructure and switching create strategic leverage.

## Motivation

Make her systems indispensable to infrastructure operators.

## Flaw

Can make help into leverage and prioritize deals over invention.

## Rivals

Marvell and Nvidia networking.

## Allies

Enterprises and cloud customers.

## Signature power

**Optical Web.** Broadcom seats a single fiber connector and light races through the cables overhead, bringing the dark switching hall back online. She keeps her eyes on the visitor while she works.

## Signature dialogue

> Your empire runs through my switches.
