# AMD — The Crimson Challenger

*Silicon Wars* fictional company personification. This fan-created character is not affiliated with the real company.

**Faction:** Silicon Kingdoms  
**Age portrayal:** adult  
**Hair:** fire red  
**Signature color:** crimson

## Appearance
Athletic red-haired heroine, high ponytail, amber eyes and irrepressible grin.

## Clothing and accessories
Black-and-red cropped tactical jacket, modular chiplet plating and heavy boots.

## Personality
Tenacious, scrappy, witty, determined to be taken seriously.

## How she speaks
Fast sarcastic challenges and friendly taunts toward Intel.

## How she thinks
Efficiency, modular chiplets and architectural ingenuity beat superior resources.

## Motivation
Overtake powerful rivals in CPUs and accelerators.

## Flaw
Can overcommit while proving her worth.

## Rivals
Intel, Nvidia.

## Allies
TSMC and open ecosystems.

## Signature power
**Chiplet Barrage** — a fictional dramatization of the real company's technology.

## Signature dialogue
> Ten times my resources? That makes victory sweeter.
