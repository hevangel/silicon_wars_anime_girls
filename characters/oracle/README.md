## Appearance
Sophisticated mature executive with warm brunette curls, red glasses and confident smile

## Clothing and equipment
Crimson tailored blazer, black silk blouse and holographic database ledger

## Personality
Strategic, competitive, formidable and commercially minded

## Speech patterns
Polished sales language and decisive negotiation

## Thinking and values
Control of databases and enterprise platforms yields durable influence

## Motivation
Build dominant data, cloud and enterprise solutions

## Dramatic flaws
Can be aggressive and overly complex

## Rivalries
IBM, Microsoft, SAP and AWS

## Alliances
Enterprise CIOs and Sun's software legacy

## Special ability
**Relational Throne** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> Information is a kingdom. I keep the keys.
