# Oracle — The Database Duchess

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Enterprise Elders |
| Age portrayal | Mature, late 50s or early 60s |
| Hair color | dark auburn |
| Brand color motif | scarlet |

## Appearance
Sophisticated executive in her late 50s or early 60s with sculpted auburn waves, amber-brown eyes, red rectangular glasses, and a confident smile.

## Clothing and equipment
Structured scarlet 1990s power blazer over a black knee-length sheath dress with a pencil-skirt hem, sheer charcoal hosiery, black pumps, gold hoops and chain, an Oracle lapel pin, and a holographic database ledger.

## Personality
Strategic, competitive, formidable and commercially minded

## Speech patterns
Polished sales language and decisive negotiation

## Thinking and values
Control of databases and enterprise platforms yields durable influence

## Motivation
Build dominant data, cloud and enterprise solutions

## Dramatic flaws
Can be aggressive and overly complex

## Rivalries
IBM, Microsoft, SAP and AWS

## Alliances
Enterprise CIOs and Sun's software legacy

## Special ability
**Relational Throne** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> Information is a kingdom. I keep the keys.
