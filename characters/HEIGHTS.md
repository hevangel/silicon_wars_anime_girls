# Canonical character heights

`roster.json` is the source of truth. Heights are barefoot standing stature, excluding hats, crowns, hair ornaments, props, and footwear. Bell Labs' seated pose is calibrated separately from her standing height.

The 2026-11-14 revision corrects the school-age cast to match the age portrayals in their profiles. The specific centimeter values are art-direction choices, not physical measurements inferred from generated images. Adult and elder values are retained.
