## Appearance
Happy elderly woman with seventies tinted glasses and playful smile

## Clothing and equipment
Bright retro geometric dress, game-controller bracelets and joystick walking stick

## Personality
Playful, competitive, mischievous and nostalgic

## Speech patterns
Always trying to start a game

## Thinking and values
Interactivity and play introduce computing to generations

## Motivation
Remind the world why computers should be fun

## Dramatic flaws
Sometimes values excitement over long-term planning

## Rivalries
Commodore and Nintendo

## Alliances
Arcade designers and vintage players

## Special ability
**Arcade Dimension** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> Enough benchmarks. Who wants to play?
