## Appearance
Dark hair streaked with red, blue, green and yellow, curious sparkling eyes

## Clothing and equipment
Research lab coat, colorful trinkets and four orbiting knowledge orbs

## Personality
Brilliant, distracted, expansive and creative

## Speech patterns
Frequently says Actually and remembers an older paper

## Thinking and values
Research and information access can power broad platforms

## Motivation
Organize knowledge and turn research into universal products

## Dramatic flaws
Too many unrelated ideas compete for attention

## Rivalries
Microsoft, OpenAI and Meta

## Alliances
Scientists, developers and Android

## Special ability
**Rainbow Searchlight** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> We researched that twelve years ago! Let me find the paper.
