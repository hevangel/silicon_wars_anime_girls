# IBM — The Iron Lady of Computing

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Enterprise Elders |
| Age portrayal | Mature, around 60 |
| Hair color | silver charcoal |
| Brand color motif | navy blue |

## Appearance
Distinguished executive around sixty with a swept silver-charcoal wavy bob, steel-blue eyes, thin gold-rimmed glasses, and dignified posture.

## Clothing and equipment
Tailored navy pinstripe double-breasted suit with peaked lapels and gently flared trousers, ivory blouse, cobalt-and-white silk scarf, IBM lapel pin, gold drop earrings and watch, and black pumps.

## Personality
Resilient, proud, strategic and patient

## Speech patterns
Formal and dryly humorous; calls younger rivals young lady

## Thinking and values
Long-lived systems need compatibility, reliability and reinvention

## Motivation
Remain influential after every technological upheaval

## Dramatic flaws
Institutional traditions sometimes slow change

## Rivalries
Oracle, Microsoft, old DEC

## Alliances
Enterprise users and researchers

## Special ability
**Mainframe Citadel** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> I have been declared obsolete more times than you have released models.
