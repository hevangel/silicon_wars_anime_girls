## Appearance
Distinguished woman around sixty, dark hair streaked silver, steel-blue eyes and dignified posture

## Clothing and equipment
Navy pinstripe suit, pearl earrings, vintage watch and mainframe-style coat

## Personality
Resilient, proud, strategic and patient

## Speech patterns
Formal and dryly humorous; calls younger rivals young lady

## Thinking and values
Long-lived systems need compatibility, reliability and reinvention

## Motivation
Remain influential after every technological upheaval

## Dramatic flaws
Institutional traditions sometimes slow change

## Rivalries
Oracle, Microsoft, old DEC

## Alliances
Enterprise users and researchers

## Special ability
**Mainframe Citadel** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> I have been declared obsolete more times than you have released models.
