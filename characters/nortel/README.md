## Personality and voice
Bold, visionary, Canadian technical pride; never cruel to those doing the work. **Worldview:** Tomorrow's traffic requires a backbone built yesterday. **Motivation:** Light the world's long-haul communications infrastructure. **Contradiction:** she can be extraordinarily competent yet treats projected demand and financing as certain rather than contingent.

## Relationships and limits
- **Rivals:** Cisco and Lucent as infrastructure competitors; dependent partnerships with carriers.
- **Allies:** Canadian research engineers, telecom carriers and optical-component partners.
- **Dependencies:** Fiber, optical components, carrier capital investment, deployment labour, physical rights-of-way.
- **Power:** **Optical Aurora**, an invented visual expression of the company's role.
- **Limit:** Cannot manufacture paying demand or survive indefinitely on expansion projections.
- **Season 2 arc:** Her brilliant engineering survives the dream's collapse in memory, people and technologies; tell the human cost without mocking workers.
