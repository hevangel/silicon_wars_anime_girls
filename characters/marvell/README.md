# Marvell — The Deep-Sea Navigator

*Silicon Wars* fictional company personification. This fan-created character is not affiliated with the real company.

- **Faction:** Connectivity Quartet
- **Age portrayal:** 20-year-old adult, in Arm and Qualcomm's cohort
- **Hair:** navy-black with a deep-teal streak
- **Signature color:** ocean teal

## Appearance

Amber eyes behind fine wire-frame glasses and a jaw-length asymmetrical bob. Her right side tucks behind the ear under a small angular Marvell clip. Her left side sweeps forward to her jaw with a deep-teal streak. She wears small teal-and-gold diamond earrings.

## Clothing and accessories

A belted, knee-length white engineering coatdress with a storm collar, rolled sleeves, a navy high-neck inset, and a Marvell emblem on the right upper sleeve. Copper piping follows the skirt hem and angular pocket seams, and narrow navy side panels give the skirt room to move. A teal harness, belt-mounted diagnostic stylus, and dark ankle work boots with teal stitching complete the outfit.

## Personality

Thoughtful, precise, and quietly ambitious. She lights up when a stubborn link comes back. She wants the older engineers to take her seriously, so she'll trace a fault alone all night before she admits she needs help.

## How she speaks

Quiet, dry, and specific. She describes optical routes as shipping lanes and link outages as lights going out along the coast.

## How she thinks

She designs optical links and custom silicon to keep data moving between processors. Every busy link should have a fallback route.

## Relationships

- [Arm](../arm/README.md) draws the blueprint, [Qualcomm](../qualcomm/README.md) volunteers for the first test run, and Marvell traces whatever fails.
- [Broadcom](../broadcom/README.md) controls the busiest junctions and remembers who needs them. Marvell takes pride in finding a second route when a link goes dark. She takes it personally when Broadcom finds a faster one.
- Cloud infrastructure builders rely on her to bring up and repair difficult links.

## Signature power

**Data Tide.** Marvell uses her diagnostic stylus to bring up a fiber link between chip packages. A cyan pulse travels through the fiber and appears on her chart like a light following a shipping lane. When a link goes dark, she can bring a fallback route online.

## Signature dialogue

> Someone must build the bridges that dragons fly across.
