# TSMC — The Quiet Queenmaker

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Foundry Guild |
| Age portrayal | Late twenties, about 28 |
| Hair color | ash silver |
| Brand color motif | pearl white, silver, and wine red |

## Appearance
Poised woman in her late twenties with an ash-silver bob, amber eyes, thin round silver glasses, and a wafer-die hairpin with a wine-red tassel.

## Clothing and equipment
Pearl-white die-grid cleanroom tunic and skirt, narrow wine-red trim and sash, silver belt, gloves, lowered mask, loupe, and pearl ankle boots.

## Personality
Quiet, disciplined, impartial and exceptionally reliable

## Speech patterns
Few words; yields and process data speak for her

## Thinking and values
Manufacturing excellence turns complicated designs into working chips

## Motivation
Make advanced silicon reliably at scale

## Dramatic flaws
Capacity and geopolitical pressures

## Rivalries
Samsung Foundry and Intel Foundry

## Alliances
ASML, Nvidia, AMD and Apple

## Special ability
**Wafer Genesis** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> You design the dream. I make it real.
