# Sun Microsystems — The Solar Archon

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Grandmother Generation |
| Age portrayal | Late sixties or seventies |
| Hair color | silver-white in a loose low bun |
| Brand color motif | royal purple and antique gold |

## Appearance
Poised woman in her late sixties or seventies with a comfortably rounded silhouette, silver-white hair in a loose low bun, warm amber-brown eyes, fine age lines and thin gold half-moon reading glasses. She looks over the glasses with a small, knowing smile.

## Clothing and equipment
Floor-length royal-purple gown with gold sunburst embroidery and server-blade skirt panels, charcoal wool coat with gold trim and lining, patterned purple-and-gold pashmina, pearl jewelry, a blue-face gold watch and low-heeled black boots. Her lapel carries the period Sun diamond ambigram. She holds a Java coffee mug and a blue MySQL dolphin charm. Her black cane has a gold Sun pommel, and her leather satchel carries a worn Unix manual.

## Personality
Warm, formidable and dryly witty. She remembers every engineer’s name and can take apart a flawed design over coffee.

## Speech patterns
Quiet, incisive questions and calmly delivered observations about Unix, networks and system design

## Thinking and values
Networks and portable software define the future

## Motivation
Keep workstation and network computing values alive

## Dramatic flaws
Sometimes idealistic about business realities

## Rivalries
IBM, Microsoft and Oracle

## Alliances
Unix developers and academics

## Special ability
**Solaris Domain** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> Sit down, dear. The network was never the problem—your architecture was.
