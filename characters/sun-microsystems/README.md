## Appearance
Younger granny with curly gray hair, joyful open-mouth laugh and energetic pose

## Clothing and equipment
Purple Sun jacket, golden sunburst T-shirt and a Solaris workstation

## Personality
Philosophical, idealistic, witty and enthusiastic

## Speech patterns
Passionate speeches about networks, Unix and open systems

## Thinking and values
Networks and portable software define the future

## Motivation
Keep workstation and network computing values alive

## Dramatic flaws
Sometimes idealistic about business realities

## Rivalries
IBM, Microsoft and Oracle

## Alliances
Unix developers and academics

## Special ability
**Solaris Domain** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> The network is the computer.
