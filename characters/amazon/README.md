## Appearance
Dark ponytail, amber eyes and determined practical expression

## Clothing and equipment
Black-orange work jacket, drone familiars and logistics utility belt

## Personality
Relentless, resourceful and customer-obsessed

## Speech patterns
Fast-fire questions about costs, scale, delivery and reliability

## Thinking and values
Efficient infrastructure and automation turn products into services

## Motivation
Serve enormous customer demand at scale

## Dramatic flaws
Optimization can become ruthless and rigid

## Rivalries
Microsoft, Google and retail rivals

## Alliances
AWS partners and suppliers

## Special ability
**Fulfillment Swarm** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> What is the cost and failure rate?
