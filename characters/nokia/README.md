## Appearance and clothing
Quilted Finnish blue utility coat with warm collar, tool belt and sturdy boots; handset badge with a period-authentic Nokia logotype. Her logo-free silhouette remains identifiable: Heavy winter-blue coat, sweeping glacier braid, a compact handset holster and a rounded shield-like silhouette. Use a historically verified company mark on a consistent accessory; the mark is removable from the identity test.

## Personality and voice
Warm, dry and remarkably unflustered. **Worldview:** Trust is built in the hand and proven every time a device survives a fall. **Motivation:** Connect ordinary people at enormous scale and across budgets. **Contradiction:** she can be extraordinarily competent yet underestimates how an operating system, developer apis and app distribution can redefine a phone.

## Relationships and limits
- **Rivals:** BlackBerry, Ericsson and Motorola; later Apple and Google's Android ecosystem.
- **Allies:** Carriers, suppliers, field technicians and Qualcomm/Arm when appropriate to the era.
- **Dependencies:** Radio standards, carrier deployment, component suppliers, software ecosystem and user experience.
- **Power:** **Connecting People**, an invented visual expression of the company's role.
- **Limit:** An army of reliable phones cannot independently supply apps or a competitive software platform.
- **Season 2 arc:** She protects wide access to mobile telephony but must decide whether preserving proven hardware practices is compatible with a software-first war.

## Performance samples
- **In command:** “It works in the rain, in the snow, and on the train. What is your excuse?”
- **Under pressure:** “Show me the failure mode, not another slogan.”
- **Private fear:** “If the world changes the rules, will they still need me?”
- **To an ally:** “I can do my part. I need you to do yours.”
- **Never says:** “My technology has no dependencies.”
