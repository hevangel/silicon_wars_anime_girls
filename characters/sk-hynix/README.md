# SK hynix — The Stack Savant

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Memory Kingdom |
| Age portrayal | adult |
| Hair color | violet-black |
| Brand color motif | red-orange gradient, charcoal, cream and gold |

## Appearance
Violet-black hair in a tall updo with a diagonal gold binyeo and three crystal cubes stacked along it, big sparkling violet eyes and a very slight pressed smile

## Clothing and equipment
Modern hanbok with correct proportions: fitted red-orange gradient jeogori ending just under the bust, vivid goreum ribbon, slim column chima with no waist belt (red-orange fading to charcoal, copper TSV lines, tiered hem), translucent wing ribbon ends, cream SK hynix sash, chest-height norigae with a copper-rod stack

## Personality
Quietly fierce, meticulous and relentlessly competitive; the focused younger sister who remembers every slight in a tabbed notebook

## Speech patterns
Soft direct sentences; remembers every slight

## Thinking and values
Memory bandwidth feeds advanced processors

## Motivation
Be the indispensable memory partner for the AI era (HBM, high-bandwidth memory, advanced packaging)

## Dramatic flaws
Perfectionistic and impatient; resents being treated as "just a memory supplier" and as her older sister's supporting character

## Rivalries
Samsung and Micron

## Alliances
NVIDIA, TSMC and AI accelerator companies

## Special ability
**Memory Tower** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> Feed the AI. At bandwidth.
