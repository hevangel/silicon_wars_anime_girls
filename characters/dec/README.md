# DEC — The Hacker Grandma

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Grandmother Generation |
| Age portrayal | senior |
| Hair color | silver gray |
| Brand color motif | burgundy |

## Appearance
Silver-haired older woman with rectangular glasses and a knowing smirk

## Clothing and equipment
Red varsity jacket, PDP-11 manual and vintage terminal keyboard

## Personality
Mischievous, independent, practical and rebellious

## Speech patterns
Friendly teasing, old-hacker humor and blunt lessons

## Thinking and values
Interactive minicomputers give individuals control

## Motivation
Preserve the spirit of understandable computers

## Dramatic flaws
Undervalued market and organizational shifts

## Rivalries
IBM, Sun and workstation vendors

## Alliances
Engineers and Unix researchers

## Special ability
**VAX Fortress** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> We did not need a whole data center to have fun.
