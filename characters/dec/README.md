# DEC — The Digital Vaklkyire

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Grandmother Generation |
| Age portrayal | Mature, around 60 |
| Hair color | silver gray |
| Brand color motif | burgundy |

## Appearance
Mature woman around sixty with short, tousled silver-gray hair, amber eyes, black rectangular glasses, fine smile lines, and a crooked grin.

## Clothing and equipment
Burgundy canvas work jacket with rolled cuffs, a three-tile DEC chest patch and a pocket pencil, charcoal open-collar work shirt, khaki trousers, a brown work belt, dark leather work boots, small gold hoops, and a fine pendant. Her lab card shows a PDP-11-style minicomputer, a green-phosphor terminal, and a circuit notebook.

## Personality
Mischievous, independent and practical. She welcomes a good argument and would rather try a new idea at the workbench than wait for a formal sign-off.

## Speech patterns
Friendly teasing, old-hacker humor and blunt lessons

## Thinking and values
Interactive minicomputers give individuals control

## Motivation
Preserve the spirit of understandable computers

## Dramatic flaws
Her faith in independent engineering teams can turn lively debate into competing projects, and she can miss shifts outside the lab.

## Rivalries
IBM, Sun and workstation vendors

## Alliances
Engineers and Unix researchers

## Special ability
**VAX Fortress** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> We did not need a whole data center to have fun.
