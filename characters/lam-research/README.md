Black-gold armor, translucent vacuum core and paired plasma swords

Disciplined, fearless, relentless and impatient with ambiguity

Short direct commands about dimensions and materials

## Thinking and values

## Motivation
Master plasma processes for advanced chips

## Dramatic flaws
Too ready to cut away a problem

## Rivalries
Applied Materials and Tokyo Electron

## Alliances
Foundries and process engineers

## Special ability
**Selective Etch** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
