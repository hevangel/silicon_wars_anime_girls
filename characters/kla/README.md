# KLA — The All-Seeing Inspector

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Equipment Guild |
| Age portrayal | adult |
| Hair color | silver |

## Appearance
Long silver hair, rotating golden microscope iris and composed expression

## Clothing and equipment
White-black inspector coat, crystalline monocle and defect ledger

## Personality
Skeptical, silent, systematic and impossible to deceive

## Speech patterns
After long silences delivers a single damning observation

## Thinking and values
Inspection and metrology make yields manageable

## Motivation
Find hidden defects before they cascade

## Dramatic flaws
An unforgiving definition of perfection

## Rivalries
Defects and unreliable processes

## Alliances
TSMC, fabs and Synopsys

## Special ability
**Electron Eye** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> Your wafer is lovely. I counted 347 defects.
