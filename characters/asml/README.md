# ASML — The Miller of Impossible Light

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Foundry Equipment Guild |
| Age portrayal | adult |
| Hair color | golden blond |
| Brand color motif | cobalt blue |

## Appearance
Adult woman with long golden-blonde waves loosely pinned under a white lace cap and cobalt ribbon, icy-blue eyes, and blue drop earrings.

## Clothing and equipment
White gathered-sleeve blouse, silver-clasped cobalt bodice, full navy skirt, ivory apron embroidered with blue tulips, white gloves, ivory socks, and black buckled shoes. A cream tulip sits at her collar and a silver windmill charm hangs at her waist.

## Personality
Eccentric, exacting, confident and technically brilliant

## Speech patterns
Cheerful explanations of extreme optical engineering

## Thinking and values
Physics and precision optics enable advanced chipmaking

## Motivation
Extend lithography into smaller and finer structures

## Dramatic flaws
Equipment is enormously complex and expensive

## Rivalries
Alternative lithography technologies

## Alliances
TSMC, Samsung and Intel

## Special ability
**EUV Mirror Halo.** Four floating silver mirrors form a windmill around a single violet point of light. She fine-tunes their alignment by hand.

## Signature line
> All we need is impossible light and several miracles.
