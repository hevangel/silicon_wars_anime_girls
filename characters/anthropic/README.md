# Anthropic — The Precautionary Prodigy

> An original *Silicon Wars* company personification. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | AI Academy |
| Age portrayal | Elementary-school child, about 9–10 |
| Hair color | Dark brown |
| Brand color motif | Terracotta |

## Look and presence

Dark-brown ringlets spill out from under a slightly crooked terracotta hard hat. The helmet has a cream reflective stripe, a small cream A sticker, and a chin strap she checks twice. Her cream rounded-collar blouse and charcoal pinafore are easy to spot under a diagonal orange reflective sash and a Class Monitor armband. She wears knee pads to school and carries a brown leather satchel with a laminated checklist clipped outside.

She holds her clipboard close to her chest, stands with her weight on her heels, and gives the action a sidelong look. One raised eyebrow usually gets there before her soft “Wait, should we?” She raises a palm close to her shoulder when it's time to stop and puts a finger to her chin when she needs to think.

## Core contradiction

**Careful but competitive.** She scans for hazards and can hold her ground when everyone else rushes ahead. She takes real pride in her gold-lettered *Claude* book and wants her plan to win on its merits. Sometimes the class has already moved on by the time she's sure.

## Habits and voice

- She adjusts her helmet whenever the curls push it out of place. There's a pencil behind her ear, a red pen in her pocket, and a strip of caution tape around her wrist.
- She keeps a bandage kit in her satchel and gives a small smile when nobody sees her help.
- She talks through the safest next step in short, measured questions. “Wait, should we test that first?”

## Relationships

- [OpenAI](../openai/README.md) was her best friend before Anthropic switched seats. They still argue about whose club rules were better. Anthropic reaches for the checklist as OpenAI starts the demonstration.
- Google and xAI challenge her in different ways. Google is both a rival and a source of compute. xAI tests her patience from the back row.
- Researchers and enterprise users back her work. Amazon supplies compute and helps keep her experiments running.

## Signature power

**Constitutional Ward** appears as a warm orange checklist with three checks · Helpful, Honest, Harmless. She uses it to pause a risky demonstration long enough to ask what might go wrong.
