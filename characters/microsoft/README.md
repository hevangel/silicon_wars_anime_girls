# Microsoft — The Azure Commander

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Platform Kingdoms |
| Age portrayal | adult |
| Hair color | deep blue |
| Brand color motif | azure |

## Appearance
Navy hair neatly tied back, glasses and a calm commanding gaze

## Clothing and equipment
Tailored azure suit with four-color pin and projected business dashboards

## Personality
Pragmatic, diplomatic, procedural and strategic

## Speech patterns
Dry humor and questions about delivery schedules

## Thinking and values
Integrate software, cloud, customers and AI into real workflows

## Motivation
Deploy sophisticated technology to organizations everywhere

## Dramatic flaws
Bureaucracy and complex platforms slow decisions

## Rivalries
Google, Amazon and Apple

## Alliances
OpenAI, businesses and developers

## Special ability
**Azure Fleet** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> Great research. Can we deploy it securely by Monday?
