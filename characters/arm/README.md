# Arm — The Pocket Architect

*Silicon Wars* fictional company personification. This fan-created character is unaffiliated with the real company.

- **Faction** — Connectivity Quartet
- **Age portrayal** — 19-year-old young adult
- **Hair** — Silver ash-blonde
- **Signature color** — Retro blue-teal

## Appearance

A petite British engineering apprentice from Cambridge with cool blue eyes and a wavy silver ash-blonde side ponytail tied with a blue-teal ribbon. She tends to size people up with one raised brow.

## Clothing and accessories

A cropped blue-teal workshop jacket with brass hardware, rolled cream cuffs, and a woven lowercase `arm` patch above her right chest pocket. She wears a cream collared shirt, charcoal pleated skirt, brass-buckled belt, and scuffed lace-up work boots. A brass mechanical pencil sits behind her ear. Her favorite demonstration prop is a thumbnail-sized working circuit board.

## Personality

Arm is observant, generous with her designs, and quietly competitive. She loves seeing what people build from her blueprints and has trouble letting them change the details. If someone calls her prototype cute, she takes it back to the bench and makes it even smaller. She keeps the first boards her friends built from her plans in a battered biscuit tin she calls her spares.

## How she speaks

Short, precise sentences with dry British humor. She delivers a challenge like a perfectly polite workshop note.

## How she thinks

One efficient architecture can serve many different devices when other builders can adapt it.

## Motivation

Get efficient processors into devices too small to run on brute force.

## Flaw

She relies on partners to build and distribute her designs, then hovers when they want to change them.

## Rivals

x86 architectures and RISC-V.

## Allies

Qualcomm, Apple and other licensees.

## Signature power

**Architecture Codex** gives other builders a blueprint they can adapt to fit their own hardware. Its strength depends on what those partners make with it.

## Signature dialogue

> Lovely. It runs. Now make it run on a battery.
