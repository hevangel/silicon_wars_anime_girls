# Arm — The Quiet Architect

*Silicon Wars* fictional company personification. This fan-created character is not affiliated with the real company.

**Faction:** Connectivity Quartet  
**Age portrayal:** adult  
**Hair:** ash blonde  
**Signature color:** sage green

## Appearance
Ash-blonde hair, light green eyes and graceful bookish posture.

## Clothing and accessories
British-academia blouse, green architectural cloak and hovering CPU blueprint.

## Personality
Diplomatic, modest and quietly influential.

## How she speaks
Soft precise sentences with understated humor.

## How she thinks
Licensing a widely adopted architecture builds an ecosystem.

## Motivation
Put efficient processors into every connected device.

## Flaw
Depends upon partners for implementation and distribution.

## Rivals
x86 architectures and RISC-V.

## Allies
Qualcomm, Apple and other licensees.

## Signature power
**Architecture Codex** — a fictional dramatization of the real company's technology.

## Signature dialogue
> I did not build your kingdom. I designed its foundations.
