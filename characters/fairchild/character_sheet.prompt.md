# Fairchild art generation record

The four assets were generated with Codex's built-in image generator. The sheets retain their native 1536 × 1024 resolution. The 1024 × 1536 reference card was saved as JPEG for the existing gallery path.

## Reference card

- Identity input: [previous Fairchild reference card](https://github.com/hevangel/silicon_wars_anime_girls/blob/67db1849cf4ebf3f295f9b35ecee7334c55d8beb/characters/fairchild/reference_card.jpg)
- Layout input: [reference card guide](../_template/layout/reference_card_layout.png)
- Emblem input: [April 1960 Fairchild Lead Wire masthead](https://semiconductormuseum.com/PreservationCollection/Fairchild_Transistors_The_First_Ten_Years.pdf#page=14)
- Output: [reference card](reference_card.jpg)

Preserve her elderly face, round glasses, silver updo with workshop pencils, and one-arm Rosie flex. Recolor her postwar work dress to deep forest green with olive polka-dot scarves and a cream sleeve patch bearing the circular Flying F. Frame her at a period semiconductor workbench with a microscope and transistors. Match the gold frame and charcoal nameplate from the guide. Use only FAIRCHILD and THE FIRST LADY OF SILICON on the nameplate.

## Character sheet

- Identity input: [new reference card](reference_card.jpg)
- Layout input: [model sheet guide](../_template/layout/model_sheet_layout.png)
- Output: [character sheet](character_sheet.png)

Use the new card as the identity and wardrobe reference. Show FRONT, THREE-QUARTER, SIDE, and BACK at a common scale and baseline. Extend the dress to below the knee with gray stockings and dark brown lace-up work shoes. Add PROUD, STERN, and AMUSED close-ups with glasses on, plus six unlabeled palette swatches. Use the standard cream-paper frame and upper-left lockup with the Flying F symbol, FAIRCHILD, THE FIRST LADY OF SILICON, and SILICON WARS.
