# Fairchild Semiconductor — The First Lady of Silicon

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Grandmother Generation |
| Age portrayal | elder |
| Hair color | white |
| Brand color motif | forest green and dark olive |

## Appearance
Wiry, visibly elderly grandmother with silver-white hair pinned up with two workshop pencils, round dark wire-rim glasses, and a proud, wry smile.

## Clothing and equipment
Forest-green postwar shirtwaist work dress with rolled cream cuffs, brass buttons and buckle, dark olive head and neck scarves with cream polka dots, a vintage Flying F sleeve patch, gray stockings, and brown lace-up work shoes. Her workbench holds an optical microscope and a tray of metal-can transistors.

## Personality
Pragmatic, bossy, protective and inventive

## Speech patterns
Commands the noisy children like an experienced forewoman

## Thinking and values
Manufacturable silicon and entrepreneurship make new empires

## Motivation
Preserve the early Silicon Valley family

## Dramatic flaws
Bitter about descendants receiving all the fame

## Rivalries
Texas Instruments and Motorola

## Alliances
Intel, AMD and startup descendants

## Special ability
**Planar Genesis** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> Intel! AMD! Stop fighting at my table!
