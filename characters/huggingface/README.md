# Hugging Face — The Open-Source Sunshine Girl

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | AI Academy |
| Age portrayal | Young adult |
| Hair color | chestnut brown |
| Brand color motif | sunny yellow |

## Appearance
Chestnut-brown curls in high twin ponytails with yellow bows, a smiling mascot clip, and warm amber-brown eyes

## Clothing and equipment
White sailor blouse with navy collar and yellow piping, navy pleated skirt with yellow trim, white socks, navy loafers, purple-and-yellow backpack, and a yellow mascot plush

## Personality
Friendly, inclusive, supportive and energetic

## Speech patterns
Welcoming explanations and effusive praise for beginners

## Thinking and values
Sharing models, tools and datasets makes AI accessible

## Motivation
Help everybody learn and build machine learning

## Dramatic flaws
Relies on many outside communities

## Rivalries
Closed model distribution platforms

## Alliances
Open AI researchers, developers and Mistral

## Special ability
**Community Hug** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> You made a model? Let's share it!
