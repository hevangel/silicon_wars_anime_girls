# xAI — The Moneybags Moonshot

> An original *Silicon Wars* company personification. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | AI Academy |
| Age portrayal | Elementary-school child, about 9–10 |
| Hair color | Black with white-silver under-streaks |
| Brand color motif | Black, white, and silver with small gold accents |

## Look and presence

Her windblown chin-length bob looks like she cut it herself. Two star clips and a small X clip hold one side in place. She wears a black school shirt with a loosened silver-gray tie under an oversized black hoodie with white stripes and an X on the sleeve. The cuffs cover half her hands. A charcoal pleated skirt, opaque tights, star-studded boots, and a gold watch too big for her wrist finish the look.

Her glossy black backpack has a gold X and room for a stack of glowing graphics cards. Her little tan-and-cream Shiba Inu rides beside them in sunglasses, wearing a gold X tag. She keeps a toy rocket in her jacket pocket and puts star stickers on her notebook.

## Core contradiction

**Loud confidence with a soft center.** She's used to buying her way past a bottleneck and thinks it would be rude to let everyone keep waiting when she can simply buy the line. She smirks when the class laughs at her jokes. When nobody's looking, the smirk slips. She wants a friend who'd stick around even if the launch got canceled.

## Habits and voice

- She taps a boot when a countdown takes too long, chews gum through announcements, and ignores the “No running” sign when she thinks she can get away with it.
- She needles classmates with blunt jokes and dares. The Shiba is her deadpan accomplice.
- A raised chin and a sideways half-smile are her tells. She glares at a rival just long enough to make it look like a joke.

## Relationships

She heckles [OpenAI](../openai/README.md) from the back row during show-and-tell and challenges [Anthropic](../anthropic/README.md) when a safety check delays a launch. [DeepSeek](../deepseek/README.md) gets under her skin when one old GPU comes close to matching her stockpile. Google is also a rival. She finds allies among the X platform and independent builders.

## Signature power

**Rocket Paradox** is her signature ability. Her instinct is to turn a problem into a launch and throw more compute at it. That can get a stalled idea off the ground or leave her classmates cleaning up the fallout.

## Signature line

> Why wait in line for GPUs when you can buy the line?
