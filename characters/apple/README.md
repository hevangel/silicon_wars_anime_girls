# Apple — The Silver Perfectionist

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Platform Kingdoms |
| Age portrayal | Adult, around 30 |
| Hair color | silver gray |
| Brand color motif | white silver |

## Appearance
Pale silver-gray layered bob with a side-swept fringe, cool slate blue-gray eyes, and the composed bearing of a woman around 30.

## Clothing and equipment
Tailored white lab coat over a black turtleneck, straight-leg white trousers, black loafers, and a silver Apple-logo necklace. She also wears silver drop earrings, a black-and-gold hair clip, and a silver-band smartwatch.

## Personality
Elegant, exacting, private and perfectionistic

## Speech patterns
Few words and long pauses; precise critiques

## Thinking and values
End-to-end integration makes products feel intuitive and beautiful

## Motivation
Create beloved products with a controlled user experience

## Dramatic flaws
Can be closed and reluctant to adopt outside solutions

## Rivalries
Microsoft, Google and Samsung

## Alliances
Arm ecosystem and application developers

## Special ability
**Seamless Domain** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> It just works.
