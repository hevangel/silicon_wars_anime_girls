# Bell Labs — The Grandmother of Transistors

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | Grandmother Generation |
| Age portrayal | elder |
| Hair color | white |
| Brand color motif | warm sepia |

## Appearance
Very elderly white-haired bespectacled scholar with deep gentle wrinkles

## Clothing and equipment
Vintage tweed research coat, bow tie, old transistor and handwritten notebooks

## Personality
Patient, wise, curious and soft-spoken

## Speech patterns
Lectures kindly and remembers foundational discoveries

## Thinking and values
Fundamental science outlives any single commercial product

## Motivation
Preserve the spirit of deep scientific research

## Dramatic flaws
Can be oblivious to commercial pressures

## Rivalries
Industrial research competitors

## Alliances
Fairchild and academic scientists

## Special ability
**Transistor Spark** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> It all started with something very small.
