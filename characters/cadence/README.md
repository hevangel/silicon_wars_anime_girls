# Cadence — The Waveform Witch

> An original *Silicon Wars* company-personification character. Unofficial fictional fan work.

| Field | Design |
|---|---|
| Faction | EDA Trinity |
| Age portrayal | adult |
| Hair color | scarlet |
| Brand color motif | magenta red |

## Appearance
Scarlet hair, bright amber eyes and a perpetually amused face

## Clothing and equipment
Red-black asymmetrical lab coat, simulation waveform sleeves and clock staff

## Personality
Hyperactive, brilliant, obsessive and playful

## Speech patterns
Rapid-fire debugging questions; insists on a reproducer

## Thinking and values
Simulation, verification and coverage expose unseen defects

## Motivation
Find every bug before silicon ships

## Dramatic flaws
Sometimes searches forever instead of shipping

## Rivalries
Synopsys and Siemens EDA

## Alliances
Chip designers and verification engineers

## Special ability
**Waveform Labyrinth** — a fictional metaphor for the real technology.

## Signature line
> Passed your regression? I found ten thousand missing cases.
