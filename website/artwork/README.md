# Artwork portfolio

Serve the repository root and open `/website/artwork/`. The index links to independent artwork pages, each free to use its own theme below the shared Silicon Wars masthead. No build or JavaScript is required to browse; the ED Walk adds optional scroll buttons and the style index adds optional info dialogs.

## Sources

- `assets/ed-walk.webp`: web-sized derivative of `art/ed-walk/ed-walk-full.png`, resized to 10,000 × 935, WebP quality 88. The detail page also links to the untouched original.
- `assets/key-art.jpg`: JPEG derivative of the regenerated [concert key art](../../art/key-art/key-art.png), matching the AMD, Intel, and Nvidia model sheets. [Prompt, provenance, and before/after review](../../art/key-art/reviews/REVIEW.md). The earlier `导出/key-art.jpg` remains preserved.
- `microsoft-styles/images/`: 16 original 784 × 1168 JPEGs from `scratchpad/microsoft-painting-styles.zip`. These are the imported source portraits.
- `microsoft-styles/images/*-studies.png`: Seventy-four ChatGPT ImageGen entries for the [style index](microsoft-styles/). Thirty-eight have four variants and thirty-six use one representative illustration each, arranged on nine comparison boards. The twelve technique boards use the classic cel portrait as a pose and costume reference. Most artistic-style entries use it as a character reference for new compositions. Memphis design, Rococo, Pointillism, Minimalism, Space Age design, Tonalism, and the studies on the Story, pattern, and perception, How pictures carry meaning, Objects, gesture, and rhythm, Work, craft, and spectacle, Space, atmosphere, and action, and Art you move around boards use the canonical [Microsoft reference card](../../characters/microsoft/reference_card.jpg) for character identity. The new Space Age scene pairs a rounded console and orange analog controls with a view of Earth. Tonalism uses a narrow blue-gray palette and a faint warm reflection on a river at dusk. The Story, pattern, and perception prompts show an office day in Egyptian registers, a shared meal with Mithila linework and borders, a walk among irises against a gold Rinpa screen, and a Photorealist view through reflective office glass. How pictures carry meaning shows a Harlem reading room linked by bands of light, an office opening inside an illuminated M, a vanitas arrangement at closing time, and a Kalighat-inspired joke about an empty executive chair. Objects, gesture, and rhythm shows a worn notebook on a chaekgeori shelf, the aftermath of a meeting through New Objectivity portraiture, a folder passed between wayang kulit puppets, and an Orphist walk among repeating electric lights. Work, craft, and spectacle uses a small-town radio repair shop, a reading nook with a climbing rose trellis pattern, a stage duet over a folder, and an idealized data-center crew. Space, atmosphere, and action shows a softly tinted street at twilight, a lobby with competing angles, a torn performance screen, and a painted box with two levels of a community computer fair. Art you move around shows a hanging mobile in a sunlit atrium, sandstone openings that frame sunrise, an ambiguous violet-lit room, and a rooftop meal at the center of a narrative quilt.

The ED Walk was recomposited after the [height correction](../../art/ed-walk/reviews/height-correction/REVIEW.md); its source walkers, costumes, and lettering remain unchanged. The ED Walk index thumbnail intentionally shows a central excerpt; its detail page offers the complete panorama and an uncropped overview.

To add work, create a directory with an `index.html`, add an index entry with descriptive alt text, and record its source here. Keep page-specific styles scoped to a body class in `style.css` (or a separate stylesheet).
