One Walk, 105 Styles.

When you are just starting to study art, comparing a watercolor landscape with an oil portrait is tough because the subjects are different. Here, Broadcom takes the exact same autumn stroll past a boutique window with a coffee cup in every single plate—so every difference you see comes purely from medium, light, line, and history.

How to read the 4-variant styles

Thirty-eight core styles include four plates to show how one medium adapts:

  1. 1 · Daylight Full-figure textbook baseline in clear autumn light.
  2. 2 · Rain / Mood Same scene shifted into rain, twilight, or cooler reflections.
  3. 3 · Close-up Cropped waist-up view so you can inspect brush grain and ink weight.
  4. 4 · Material The physical quirks—paper tooth, ink bleed, or impasto—turned up high.
