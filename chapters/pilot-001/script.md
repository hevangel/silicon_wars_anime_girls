# Silicon Wars · Pilot 001 · The Compatibility Test

**Logline** · Intel’s Citadel runs on old code. AMD can bridge the moat, but the test begins only when Nvidia lends a hand.

1. **The Citadel.** A processor-shaped fortress looms over a dry moat lined with circuit traces. A steel gate closes off the entrance. Silent establishing shot.
2. **The rule.** At the gate, Intel lifts her wafer heirloom. **Intel:** “The old code stays.”
3. **A new bridge.** AMD smirks and calls a string of chiplets into the air. **AMD:** “Then I’ll build around it.”
4. **A little leverage.** Nvidia gets under the massive gate and shoulders it up, steadying the edge with one palm. **Nvidia:** “Need a hand?”
5. **Chiplets lock.** AMD’s modules snap across the moat and meet the open gate. **SFX:** “KLIK KLIK!”
6. **The progress bar.** Inside, AMD, Nvidia, and Intel gather around the test console with mugs. The screen reads “COMPATIBILITY CHECK: 1%”. **Intel:** “Now we wait.” **AMD:** “I brought coffee.”

The bridge gets them through the door. The test is still ahead.
