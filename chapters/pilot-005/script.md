**Logline** · Two challengers, one ball, and a queen who does not need to try.

## Page 001 · Beach Volleyball (single 4:3 panel)

Sun, sand, and a rally going badly. Nvidia rises on the far side of the net and puts the ball straight past AMD's dive toward Intel's face. Read right to left along the top, then down:

- **Nvidia:** "Two against one. Still not close."
- **Intel:** "Not the face!"
- **AMD:** "Mine! MINE!"

The swimsuits carry over from the [beach concept](../../pilot-003/README.md): royal blue with gold trim, red and black athletic wear, black with green circuit traces. Crown, goggles, braid and colour palettes come straight from the character sheets.
