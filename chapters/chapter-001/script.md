# Compute Triad · Page 1

1. **Wide establishing shot.** A semiconductor conference lab. Intel stands at the demo bench with a wafer. AMD waits by the display. No dialogue.
2. **Medium shot.** Intel lifts the wafer so the audience can see it. “The next generation starts here.”
3. **Close-up.** AMD smirks, her red ponytail in silhouette. “Competition makes us stronger.”
4. **Extreme close-up.** Intel's sapphire eye shifts toward AMD. No dialogue.
5. **Low angle.** Nvidia swings the lab door open. Green light cuts across the floor. Sound effect “CLACK”.
6. **Silent reaction.** Wide enough to read the three silhouettes. Intel and AMD freeze with their eyes on Nvidia.
