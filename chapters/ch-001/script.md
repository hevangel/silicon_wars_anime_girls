# Silicon Wars · Chapter 001 · Who Owns the Table

**Logline** · The compatibility test is stuck at 1%. Intel, AMD, and Nvidia each blame someone else, until a stern voice from the head of the table reminds them where they came from and what they cannot do alone.

**Continues** [Pilot 001](../pilot-001/script.md). Reading order is right to left, top to bottom. Panel IDs (`p1-01` = page 1, panel 1) are stable; reuse them in storyboards, briefs, and lettering.

**Cast** Intel, AMD, Nvidia, Fairchild (first appearance), TSMC (last-page reveal).

**Theme beat** Interdependence. Every queen in this chapter fails because of something she cannot make herself.

**Continuity guards** (from the [bible](../../bible/story_bible.md))
- Nvidia: strongest arms in the cast; the CUDA Dragon is a hologram, never part of her costume.
- Intel: crown and coat stay formal. She is flustered, not slapstick.
- Fairchild: rolled sleeves, red bandana, glasses. Do not copy Bell Labs' silhouette.
- Nobody is undressed or fanservice-framed; this is a seinen negotiation comedy.

---

## Page 1 · 1% (6 panels)

- **p1-01** Wide. Citadel test console room, the same console, the same mugs. Screen: "COMPATIBILITY CHECK: 1%". Intel, AMD, and Nvidia sit around it. Silence. Clock on the wall.
  - **SFX:** tick… tick…
- **p1-02** Close on the progress bar. Still 1%.
  - **AMD** (off): "It's been forty minutes."
- **p1-03** AMD, chin on her fist. **AMD:** "My bridge is perfect. So it's your code."
- **p1-04** Intel, dignified, not looking at her. **Intel:** "My code is flawless. It has been flawless for forty years."
- **p1-05** Nvidia, leaning back, mug raised. **Nvidia:** "Darling, it's neither. It's the *test* that's slow."
- **p1-06** All three glare at the screen. It beeps once.
  - **Screen:** "BRIDGE SUPPLY: INSUFFICIENT"
  - **AMD:** "…Supply?"

## Page 2 · Blame Round (5 panels)

- **p2-01** AMD points at Intel. **AMD:** "You ordered the moat. Moats need materials!"
- **p2-02** Intel, stiff. **Intel:** "The Citadel's foundations are *historic.* One does not 'order' history."
- **p2-03** Nvidia stands, a grin. **Nvidia:** "I'll just lift whatever's missing."
- **p2-04** She presses her palm to the console. Nothing happens. The bar does not move.
  - **SFX:** bzzt.
- **p2-05** Nvidia, quietly stunned, hand still on the console. **Nvidia:** "…It's not something I can lift."

## Page 3 · The Dragon Tries (splash, 1 panel)

- **p3-01** Splash. Nvidia, frustrated, summons the CUDA Dragon behind her: huge, green, holographic, roaring at the console. The console shows the same message. AMD is flung back by the wind; Intel clutches her crown.
  - **Nvidia:** "CUDA! Fix it!"
  - **Screen:** "BRIDGE SUPPLY: INSUFFICIENT"
  - **SFX:** GWOOOOH.

*Read as the comedy peak of Act 1: the strongest character in the room demonstrates her power cannot fix this.*

## Page 4 · Stop Fighting at My Table (6 panels)

- **p4-01** The door slams. A short silhouette, rolled sleeves, red bandana. **Voice:** "STOP FIGHTING AT MY TABLE!"
  - **SFX:** BAM.
- **p4-02** Reveal: **Fairchild**, glasses glinting, a ladle in one hand. The three straighten as if caught.
- **p4-03** Intel, whisper. **Intel:** "Grandmother Fairchild."
- **p4-04** AMD, whisper. **AMD:** "We thought you retired."
- **p4-05** Fairchild, flat. **Fairchild:** "Retired? I *run* this room. You two grew up under my roof."
- **p4-06** Fairchild taps the console. **Fairchild:** "I wrote this test before either of you had a name."

## Page 5 · The Real Test (5 panels)

- **p5-01** Fairchild sets down the ladle and paces. **Fairchild:** "The test doesn't check your code."
- **p5-02** Close on her glasses. **Fairchild:** "It checks whether you can stand next to each other and still get a chip made."
- **p5-03** Intel, chin up, wounded pride. **Intel:** "We can do that."
- **p5-04** AMD, crossed arms. **AMD:** "Obviously."
- **p5-05** Fairchild, deadpan. **Fairchild:** "Then why is the bar at one percent?"

## Page 6 · What Nobody Can Make (6 panels)

- **p6-01** Fairchild points at the screen. **Fairchild:** "Look at the message."
- **p6-02** Screen, large: "BRIDGE SUPPLY: INSUFFICIENT". Under it, smaller: "WAFER ALLOCATION: NONE".
- **p6-03** AMD reads it aloud. **AMD:** "Wafers. My chiplets need wafers."
- **p6-04** Intel, reluctant. **Intel:** "Mine too. I merely have… other arrangements."
- **p6-05** Nvidia, arms folded. **Nvidia:** "Every design I've ever made needed one."
- **p6-06** Fairchild, quiet. **Fairchild:** "And who makes the wafers?"

## Page 7 · The Quiet One (5 panels)

- **p7-01** Silence. All three look at the floor.
- **p7-02** Intel, small voice. **Intel:** "…I have my own fabs."
- **p7-03** Fairchild raises one eyebrow. **Fairchild:** "Do they have capacity for *three* queens' orders at the leading edge?"
- **p7-04** Intel, stung. **Intel:** "…Not this quarter."
- **p7-05** AMD smirks, reflexively. Intel and Nvidia both look at her. AMD deflates. **AMD:** "I don't have any either."

## Page 8 · Tea (6 panels, final)

- **p8-01** A soft knock. The door opens slowly.
- **p8-02** **TSMC** stands in the doorway in her artisan uniform, holding a tea tray with four cups. Calm. Unhurried. **TSMC:** "I heard the table was loud."
- **p8-03** Intel, AMD, and Nvidia stare. A beat.
- **p8-04** TSMC sets down the tray. **TSMC:** "You've all been asking for the same thing without saying it."
- **p8-05** She hands the first cup to Fairchild. Fairchild takes it. **Fairchild:** "Sit."
- **p8-06** Final: the four sit around one table. The screen behind them has moved. **Screen:** "COMPATIBILITY CHECK: 2%".
  - **Caption:** "It will take a lot more than tea."

## Hooks for Chapter 002

- Nvidia now knows she cannot feed the Dragon without HBM. She asks TSMC who makes it, and TSMC names Micron and SK hynix.
- Intel and TSMC hold a private look. The foundry rivalry is not over; it has only paused for tea.
- Fairchild tells AMD and Intel they have a "homework assignment." They have no idea what it is yet.
