Silicon Wars · art direction study

Arm in every style

One character, one pose, 105 styles. Each plate redraws Arm, the Pocket Architect, walking away with her folded umbrella — the pose of the supplied reference — so that only the medium changes. Styles whose subject is a visual language rather than a material are allowed to rebuild the composition around the same girl, street and season.

105 styles · 219 plates · 35 rendered · character sheet used as the identity reference for every plate · prompts and hashes recorded beside each render.

Manga panel

Anime cel shading

Japanese animation cel art: clean tapered ink line art, flat matte colour fills, hard two-tone cel shadows with crisp boundaries, small white specular highlights on hair and boots, painted background in a slightly softer hand than the figure.

Manga ink and screentone

Black-and-white manga: brush-and-pen line art with varying line weight, hatching for shadow, dot screentone and tone-on-tone gradient screen for the skirt, sky and stonework; pure black solids, no colour, white paper ground.

Digital painting

Digital painting in the modern conceptual-illustration manner: soft opaque brush strokes, lost-and-found edges, layered atmospheric depth, warm rim light against cool ambient shadow, deliberate overpainting at the silhouette.

Pixel art

Hand-placed pixel art on a strict grid with a limited indexed palette, one-pixel dark outlines, ordered dithering for gradients, no anti-aliasing, no sub-pixel detail; readable silhouette at thumbnail size.

Drawing, paint, and paper

Watercolour

Transparent watercolour on cold-pressed cotton paper: wet-in-wet blooms and backruns, pigment pooling at the edges, reserved white paper for the brightest lights, soft graphite underdrawing left visible, muted earth-and-teal palette.

Oil painting

Oil on stretched linen: loaded opaque brushwork, scumbled half-tones, glazed shadow passages, visible impasto ridges in the light, warm-cool colour vibration, soft feathered edges against crisp accents.

Gouache

Opaque gouache: flat matte blocks of colour laid over one another, dry-brush passages where the paper tooth shows, crisp shapes with soft chalky edges, slightly chalk-lightened pastel palette, no glossy highlights.

Coloured pencil

Waxy coloured pencil on fine tooth paper: layered crosshatched strokes, visible individual pencil marks, burnished areas where the wax is pressed smooth, paper tooth catching light in the open passages, gentle tonal build rather than flat fill.

Charcoal drawing

Vine and compressed charcoal on toned paper: smudged soft masses, dragged and lifted highlights with a kneaded eraser, sharp compressed-charcoal accents, dusty halos around the darks, a few white chalk touches.

Soft pastel

Soft pastel on tinted pastel paper: powdery blended passages, sticks laid on their side for broad strokes, crisp edges cut with the tip, the paper's colour breathing through the gaps, velvety luminous light.

Paper collage

Cut-paper collage: shapes torn and scissor-cut from coloured and printed paper, layered with visible edges and soft drop shadows, misregistered overlaps, a few halftone and map fragments, glue sheen, no drawn line where paper can do the work.

Acrylic painting

Acrylic on canvas: fast-drying flat opaque strokes with crisp plastic edges, a hard matte film, some thin translucent washes over dry paint, uniform non-yellowing surface, brighter and flatter than oil.

Egg tempera

Egg tempera on gessoed panel: tiny parallel hatched strokes layered over a white ground, luminous translucent colour, hard eggshell-smooth surface, crisp dry edges, the light seeming to come from inside the paint.

Buon fresco

Buon fresco: pigment in lime plaster while it is still wet, matte chalky colour fused into the wall, broad decisive strokes, faint giornata joins between sections of plaster, slight lime bloom, the wall's texture under everything.

Graphite drawing

Graphite on smooth Bristol board: silver-grey tonal range built from hatching and smudging, sharp 2H detail against soft 6B masses, erased highlights, a whisper of graphite dust, no colour at all.

Pen and ink illustration

Pen-and-ink illustration: flexible nib line of swelling and tapering weight, dense crosshatching and stipple for tone, deliberate hatching direction, pure black ink on white, every value made of line.

Ink wash

Ink wash on sized paper: single ink diluted through many greys, wet blooms and tide marks, dry brush dragged over texture, one dark calligraphic accent, a large unpainted breathing space.

Prints, photographs, and camera vision

Woodblock-inspired print

Woodblock print: flat areas of unmodulated colour from separate blocks, a firm keyblock outline that thins and swells, visible woodgrain and goma-zuri speckle, bleed of the water-based ink into the paper edge, a small registry margin.

Ukiyo-e

Ukiyo-e woodblock print in the manner of the floating world: elegant flowing keyblock line, flat Prussian-blue and beni-e reds with bokashi sky gradation, patterned textile detail, a cartouche panel left empty, the figure stylised to the kanzōshi silhouette.

Pictorialist photography

Pictorialist photograph circa 1900: soft-focus gum-bichromate or bromoil printing, hazy diffusion, warm sepia-to-platinum tonality, visible paper texture and brush marks in the emulsion, a vignetted frame.

Photorealism

Photorealistic rendering: 50 mm lens at eye level, shallow depth of field, physically accurate skin, wool, denim and wet stone, natural overcast light, fine film grain, absolutely no visible brushwork.

Linocut

Linocut relief print: bold gouge marks, large uncut black or single-colour masses, white lines where the block was cut away, slight ink squash at the edges, visible linoleum texture in the flat areas.

Copperplate etching

Copperplate etching: bitten lines of varying fineness, dense crosshatching in the shadows, occasional foul-biting, plate mark around the image, warm off-white laid paper.

Lithography

Stone lithography: greasy crayon-and-tusche drawing, granular crayon texture on the stone, soft wash-like tonal passages, faint stone grain in the flats, printer's registration, slightly grey-black ink on cream paper.

Screenprinting

Screenprint: a few flat spot-colour layers, hard-edged misregistration, ink pushed through the mesh leaving a faint squeegee streak, opaque colour over colour, no gradients except where a halftone dot is visible.

Cyanotype

Cyanotype contact print: monochrome Prussian-blue image on textured watercolour paper, white where objects blocked the light, brush-stroked emulsion border, sun-bleached tonal range, one or two faint chemical stains.

Documentary photography

Black-and-white documentary photograph: 35 mm reportage, available light, grainy high-contrast print, caught mid-gesture, the street's real clutter left in, a slight tilt and no staging.

Ancient Egyptian wall painting

Mughal album painting

Classic Maya painted ceramics

Classic Maya painted ceramic: slip-painted calligraphic line in red and black on a cream ground, the image wound around a cylindrical vessel, a band of glyph-like blocks at the rim, warm terracotta and iron-oxide palette, slight erosion.

Medieval European manuscript illumination

Medieval illumination: egg-tempera and gold leaf on vellum, a framed miniature with a burnished gold ground, diaper pattern in the border, Gothic script-like rubric marks left decorative, flat bright colour, punched gold tooling.

Trompe-l'oeil: the scene painted to fool the eye as a physical object — a torn poster or a print pinned to a board, painted nails and their cast shadows, a painted curling corner and a painted fold, hyper-precise detail, the wall's own surface continuing around the illusion.
