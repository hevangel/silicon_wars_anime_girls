# Practical studio cosplay prompt

This is **buildable convention-costume photography**, not CGI fantasy or unbuildable key art. Never mimic a real executive/celebrity. Preserve adult identity via approved manga sheet and style override.

## Prompt assembly (four independently editable blocks)

1. **Character:** AMD red braided high ponytail / amber eyes / black-crimson chiplet design / headphones; or Intel ice-blue long hair / sapphire crown / blue-cream military coat / wafer. Their default `continuity.yaml` remains the visual source.
2. **Costume tier:** `maker` (off-the-rack modifications, sewable appliqués, EVA foam, modest work) or `showpiece` (custom tailored pieces, careful lightweight thermoplastic and staged paint); quote a price only from sourced material invoices.
3. **Camera / lighting:** editorial full-body studio, neutral grey seamless background; 50–85mm human perspective, soft key and clean shadows; coherent exposure. Capture 3-view wardrobe, wig/makeup, material closeups, prop dimension sheets separately.
4. **Output / provenance:** consent for any actual performer, source reference hashes, garment IDs, construction notes, photo/model identifier, neutral full-body and detail crops, pending review.

### AMD — maker cosplay prompt

Original adult fictional cosplayer (not a known person) wearing a crimson-and-black altered jacket, **red braided high ponytail**, headphones around her neck, fingerless gloves, cargo pants and lightweight attached foam chiplet armor. Show seams, velcro placement and supported costume panels, visible boots on studio floor. Full body front/side/back on a neutral cyclorama. Show the full braided hair path and armor articulation. Practical photography with cloth folds and believable material shine.

### Intel — showpiece cosplay prompt

Original adult fictional cosplayer wearing royal-blue/cream tailored military coat with button closures, gold cord and shoulder epaulette support, a safely secured **unbranded pointed sapphire crown**, long light-blue wig, white gloves, and a safe wafer-shaped prop. Small Intel Inside insignia at the lower left skirt front, NOT on crown. Photographed head-to-boot in studio with separately pictured seam, collar, gold trim, crown mounts and skirt hem.

## Negative constraints

NO floating unsupported pauldrons, magic lightning, impossible cloth, giant prop weapons, safety-hazard sharp props, sexualized or youthful appearance, body paint substituting for actual fabric without disclosure, logos floating in midair, missing footwear, over-composited glossy CGI, fake item prices, unapproved model identity, fake documentary captions.

## Additional production notes

Photoreal image generation can hide physical contradictions. Human costume-maker must verify closures, balance, comfort and movement before any physical build. Photographs are visual references only. Preserve actor face independently of garment changes if reusing cinematic casting identity.
