# Hyperreal fantasy film key art — prompt and layers

**Casting lock:** inherit `actor_id` and face/complexion/hairline from each `live_action_cinematic.yaml`. These are **original fictional adult performers**, not any real executive or celebrity. No new actor is created by lighting or mood variation.

## Prompt blocks

**Identity** (invariant): current approved manga identity plus actor casting face; adult, anatomy unchanged. AMD: red braided high ponytail, amber eyes, black-crimson chiplet gear and headphones. Intel: long light-blue hair, sapphire eyes, pointed **unbranded** crown, blue/cream military coat, wafer heirloom and Intel Inside at skirt hem.

**Costume** (editable physical vs simulated): label each armor/fabric piece that is actually wearable and each luminous ornament that is CGI; physical pieces must have fasteners, weight and believable material contact. Dramatic armor may extend silhouette only when secondary support or simulated material is explicit.

**VFX** (editable independently): clear foreground/mid/background passes, camera match, emissive highlights, faint bounce light, alpha RGBA plates and unobstructed facial identity. High saturation belongs in colored circuitry, never skin recoloring.

**Scene** (editable): lens, action, expression, choreography, shallow or deep DOF, atmospheric perspective, spark intensity and camera roll, separately from identity.

### Intel single-character master

Original fictional adult actress in an imperial blue-cream coat and **sapphire crown** holding a wafer heirloom. Same face and normal eye anatomy as prior neutral casting. Photo-real photographed armor with stitching and shadows; an enormous translucent **sapphire x86 Citadel** emerges behind and to her left, projected architecture casts soft blue bounce onto the ground. Isolate citadel and glow as transparent VFX plates; clean base actor and costume image retained.

### AMD single-character master

Original fictional adult red-braided high ponytail, athletic confident actress with headphones and modular crimson/black gear, same approved face as neutral casting. A storm of **crimson chiplets** arcs outward in the direction of her punch, clearly composited behind or in front of her as individually editable red/orange effects. Preserve face, braid, headphone geometry; supported plates attach to jacket; motion trails don't replace the costume.

### Two-character showdown

At the x86 Citadel court, Intel projects a sapphire defensive wall while AMD's modular crimson array impacts it. Camera 35–50mm, shared perspective and grounded footsteps. Provide separately named layers: `actor`, `wardrobe`, `citadel_fx`, `chiplet_fx`, `glow`, `atmosphere`. Each plate receives a clear alpha path and has the same pixel dimensions; check source-to-output synchronization.

## Negative instructions

No new faces when VFX enabled; no real-person lookalikes; no merged flattened effects as only editable source; no copyright character/games; no impossible unexplained costume physics; no skin smoothing/glowing plastic; no hollow floating feet; no inconsistent light/shadow; no logos on crown; no fantasy replacing character identity; no fabricated "approved" claim.

## Provenance

For each source plate record model/runtime version, seed if exposed, full prompt, negative prompt, source image hash, actor/wardrobe IDs, camera settings, FX palette, lens, layer role and human review status. Compositing source files are separate; final key art is an export, not an authoritative reference sheet.
