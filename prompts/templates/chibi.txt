Silicon Wars prompt template — style variant: chibi

Assemble every generation prompt from three blocks. The identity block is
the same one used for default_manga; only the style block changes. Per-
character preserve / simplify lists live under `style_variants.chibi` in
each character's continuity.yaml.

[BASE IDENTITY BLOCK]
Draw {{NAME}} from Silicon Wars. Preserve her identity: {{IDENTITY}}
(persona: {{PERSONALITY}}; signature prop / power: {{SIGNATURE_PROP}}).

[STYLE BLOCK — chibi]
Render her as a super-deformed chibi character: large head, tiny body,
head-to-body ratio about 1:2.5, short simplified limbs, small rounded hands
and feet, simplified costume details but keep the major motifs and signature
accessories recognizable, rounded cute shapes, clean manga line art, minimal
shading, large simplified eyes, simple and very expressive mouth shapes,
highly exaggerated expression, cute comedic pose language. Avoid realistic
anatomy.

[SCENE BLOCK]
{{POSE, EXPRESSION, CAMERA, ENVIRONMENT for this panel or asset}}

Worked example (Intel):
Draw Intel from Silicon Wars in chibi style. Preserve her identity: adult
woman personification of Intel, long platinum-silver hair, sapphire eyes,
navy-and-silver imperial military coat, blue color theme, wafer heirloom
motif, proud and composed personality. Render her as a super-deformed chibi
character with a large head, tiny body, short limbs, simplified costume
details, rounded cute shapes, clean manga line art, minimal shading, and
exaggerated expression. She is standing with tiny regal posture and a smug
expression, holding a glowing wafer badge.

Worked example (AMD):
Draw AMD from Silicon Wars in chibi style. Preserve her identity: fire-red
high ponytail, amber eyes, black-and-red tactical jacket, chiplet armor
motif, athletic challenger personality, witty and energetic attitude. Render
her as a super-deformed chibi character with a large head, tiny body,
simplified armor, bold silhouette, rounded cute style, minimal shading, and
exaggerated expression. She is leaning forward with a mischievous grin and a
tiny fist-pump, with small floating chiplet motifs around her.
