Silicon Wars prompt template — style variant: default_manga

Assemble every generation prompt from three blocks. Never bake a style
variant into the identity block: identity comes from the character's
continuity.yaml and stays identical across variants.

[BASE IDENTITY BLOCK]
Draw {{NAME}} from Silicon Wars. Preserve her identity: {{IDENTITY}}
(persona: {{PERSONALITY}}; signature prop / power: {{SIGNATURE_PROP}}).

[STYLE BLOCK — default_manga]
Render in the primary Silicon Wars manga style: standard anime adult
proportions, head-to-body ratio about 1:6.5 to 1:7, clean manga line art,
cel shading or manga screentone, high detail, moderate expression
exaggeration, heroic and dramatic pose language.

[SCENE BLOCK]
{{POSE, EXPRESSION, CAMERA, ENVIRONMENT for this panel or asset}}

Worked example (Intel):
Draw Intel from Silicon Wars in the default manga style. Preserve her
identity: adult woman personification of Intel, long light-blue hair,
sapphire eyes, unbranded sapphire crown, royal-blue and cream military coat
with gold epaulettes, white gloves, wafer heirloom, proud and composed
personality. Render her at standard anime adult proportions with clean manga
line art, cel shading, and high costume detail. She stands in a heroic
three-quarter pose, offering a glowing data cable, expression mildly
impressed.
