Task category: Illustration, Concept Art, and Narrative Visuals
Intended use: one plate in a cross-era art-style study sheet of the Silicon Wars character Arm; a technique reference for the project, not a story panel
Core request: redraw Arm, in the reference pose, as a study in Art Nouveau
Character, identity must not drift: Arm, the Pocket Architect: a petite 19-year-old British engineering apprentice, cool blue eyes, a long wavy silver ash-blonde high side ponytail tied with a blue-teal ribbon, layered bangs, a brass mechanical pencil tucked behind her right ear beside the ponytail, one eyebrow slightly raised and a wry half-smile. She wears a cropped blue-teal workshop jacket with brass hardware, stitched chest pockets and rolled cream cuffs, a small cream patch above her right chest pocket, a cream button-front shirt, a charcoal knee-length pleated skirt with a brass-buckled belt, and dark ankle-height lace-up work boots.
Subject, pose and staging: Her back is to the viewer: she is walking away down the path, seen entirely from behind at a three-quarter back angle, so the viewer sees her back, the back of her head and one cheek as she turns to look over her right shoulder toward the viewer. She is mid-stride on a pale stone footpath between black iron railings; her right hand carries a folded black umbrella low at her side, a tan shoulder bag hangs from her left shoulder, her left arm is lifted slightly. Beyond her: pale European stone roofs with chimneys and a small domed tower, autumn trees in orange and ochre, a broad pale sky. Tall thin vertical accents frame both edges. Do not turn her to face the camera.
Style or medium: Art Nouveau poster and panel: whiplash sinuous contour, a heavy decorative outline, muted sage-olive and dusty violet with gold accents, hair and drapery resolved into ornamental tendrils, stylised foliage framing the figure, flat colour inside the line, lithographic texture.
Variant 4 of 4 — material-extreme: the same subject and pose with the medium's signature artifacts pushed to the front: the surface, edge behaviour and texture become the subject
Composition and orientation: portrait plate, one single figure, full figure except where the variant calls for a closer crop
Must preserve: Arm's identity: silver ash-blonde side ponytail and blue-teal ribbon, cool blue eyes, brass pencil behind the ear, blue-teal cropped jacket with the cream chest patch, cream shirt, charcoal pleated skirt, dark lace-up boots.
Must not include: no other characters, no speech balloons, no caption or panel text, no signature, no watermark, no gallery frame, no colour swatch strip, no character-sheet turnaround or multiple views of the figure
Medium fidelity: the plate must read as the named medium at a glance. Do not fall back on anime cel shading, glossy digital rendering or clean uniform line art unless that is the medium being studied.
